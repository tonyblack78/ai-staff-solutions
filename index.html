Before you hire

What happens if it gets something wrong?

Every role has escalation rules — anything outside its confidence threshold gets routed to a human teammate instead of guessed at. You set where that line is.

Can I customize a role for my business?

Yes. Pricing above is a starting point for the standard version of each role; most teams tune tone, scope, and integrations during onboarding.

What if I need a role that isn't listed?

Tell us what the job involves and we'll scope a custom build. New roles ship most months.

How fast can I actually get one running?

Most roles are live in your workspace within days of kickoff, not weeks — the bulk of the time is connecting your existing tools.
